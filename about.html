We believe good design starts with listening.

Gridline Home Ideas is an independent editorial publication exploring interior design, architecture, materials, and modern living — with a focus on knowledge over trends, and substance over style.

Our mission

We started Gridline because we were tired of surface-level design coverage. Too much of what passes for interior journalism is about shopping links and fleeting trends. We wanted to create something different: a publication that treats design as a discipline — one that rewards curiosity, patience, and a willingness to understand how buildings and rooms actually work.

Our articles are written by practising designers, architects, and journalists. We go deep on the details that matter: how a material ages, how light moves through a space, how a floor plan supports the way people live. We don’t chase clicks. We chase clarity.

Gridline is reader-supported and editorially independent. We don’t accept sponsored content, and we don’t let advertisers influence what we publish.

What guides us

  • 01
    Knowledge over trends

    We explain the why, not just the what. Our guides are built to be useful for years, not weeks.

  • 02
    Material honesty

    We celebrate materials that age with grace and reject the synthetic and the disposable.

  • 03
    Editorial independence

    No sponsored posts. No pay-to-play. Our only allegiance is to our readers.

  • 04
    Practical optimism

    Good design is within reach. We show you how to make better choices, whatever your budget.

About Gridline Home Ideas

Gridline Home Ideas is an independent editorial publication dedicated to the art and craft of interior design and architecture. We believe that good design is not about following trends, but about understanding how people live.

Our writers and editors are practising designers, architects, and journalists who care deeply about the details — from the way light falls across a room to the precise specification of a door handle.
