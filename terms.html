Terms Of Privacy

These terms govern your use of Gridline Home Ideas, our editorial independence, and how we handle privacy. We keep them clear and readable — just like our journalism.

Last updated: 1 March 2025

1. Our editorial promise

Gridline Home Ideas is an independent editorial publication. We are not a shopping site, a PR channel, or a content farm. Everything we publish is written by or with practising designers, architects, and journalists. We do not accept sponsored articles, paid placements, or “pay-to-play” coverage. Any affiliate links, if used, are clearly disclosed and never influence our editorial judgment.

We strive for accuracy, but design and building regulations vary by region and change over time. Always consult a qualified professional before making structural, electrical, or plumbing decisions.

2. Privacy & data

We respect your privacy and collect as little data as possible. When you visit Gridline, we may collect:

  • Log data — such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used only to understand site performance and improve our content.
  • Search queries — if you use the search bar, we process your query to return relevant articles. We do not store search histories.
  • Contact information — if you email us or use our contact form, we keep your message only as long as needed to respond.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not run invasive tracking scripts. For more details, please see our Privacy Policy and Cookies Policy.

3. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your data.
  • Object to processing or request that we restrict it.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@gridlinehomeideas.com. We will respond within 30 days.

4. Content & intellectual property

All original text, graphics, and photography on Gridline Home Ideas are the property of Gridline Editorial or our contributors, unless otherwise credited. You may quote short excerpts with a link back to the original article. Republishing full articles without written permission is not allowed.

Some images are used under license from Unsplash or other providers. Their terms apply to those assets. If you believe your work has been used incorrectly, please contact us and we will rectify it promptly.

5. User contributions

We welcome thoughtful comments and reader questions. By submitting content (comments, emails, or story tips), you grant us a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to publish and edit your contribution for clarity and length. We reserve the right to remove any content that is abusive, spammy, or off-topic.

“Good design starts with listening — and so does good publishing. We’re here to learn from our readers as much as to inform them.”

6. Third-party links

Our articles may link to external websites, products, or resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to read their terms before engaging.

7. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. We will not reduce your rights without clear notice.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about these terms, your privacy, or our editorial policies, please reach out:

  • Email: privacy@gridlinehomeideas.com
  • Post: Gridline Editorial, 42 Design Street, Copenhagen, Denmark
  • Contact page: gridlinehomeideas.com/contact

These terms are provided in plain language for clarity. They do not replace your statutory rights under applicable law.

About Gridline Home Ideas

Gridline Home Ideas is an independent editorial publication dedicated to the art and craft of interior design and architecture. We believe that good design is not about following trends, but about understanding how people live.

Our writers and editors are practising designers, architects, and journalists who care deeply about the details — from the way light falls across a room to the precise specification of a door handle.
