Before you write

Pitching an article

We prefer concise pitches (200–300 words) that outline the angle, the intended audience, and why it belongs on Gridline. Please include links to previous work if available.

Corrections

Accuracy matters. If you spot an error in one of our articles, please email us with the URL and the specific correction. We update articles promptly when needed.

Press & PR

We do not accept unsolicited product samples or sponsored posts. For press releases, please email our editorial team — we review everything but cannot guarantee coverage.
