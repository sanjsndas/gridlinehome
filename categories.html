About Gridline Home Ideas

Gridline Home Ideas is an independent editorial publication dedicated to the art and craft of interior design and architecture. We believe that good design is not about following trends, but about understanding how people live.

Our writers and editors are practising designers, architects, and journalists who care deeply about the details — from the way light falls across a room to the precise specification of a door handle.
