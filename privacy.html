Privacy Policy

How Gridline Home Ideas collects, uses, and safeguards your personal information. We believe in transparency and keeping things simple.

Last updated: 1 January 2025

Gridline Home Ideas ("we", "us", "our") operates the website www.gridlinehomeideas.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

In short: We collect only what we need to run the publication, we never sell your data, and we use minimal third‑party services.

1. Information we collect

a. Information you provide voluntarily

When you contact us through our contact form, subscribe to our newsletter, or email us directly, you may provide:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The content of your message (including any attachments or links)
  • Any other information you choose to share

b. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we collect certain technical information automatically when you visit the Site. This may include:

  • IP address (anonymised where possible)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring website or source
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)

This data is used in aggregate to understand how the Site is used and to improve the experience for all readers. We do not use it to identify individual visitors.

c. Cookies and similar technologies

We use a small number of cookies to help the Site function properly and to understand basic usage patterns. For full details, please see our Cookies Policy.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries — when you contact us, we use your details to reply.
  • To send newsletters — if you subscribe, we use your email to send you editorial updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To improve the Site — aggregated analytics help us understand what content is useful and how to make the Site better.
  • To maintain security — we may use technical data to detect and prevent abuse or unauthorised access.
  • To comply with legal obligations — in rare cases, we may need to retain or disclose information if required by law.

We do not use your personal information for automated decision‑making or profiling.

3. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), our processing of your personal data is governed by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — for newsletter subscriptions and non‑essential cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — for responding to enquiries, improving the Site, and ensuring security. We balance these interests against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to process or retain data by law.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — we use trusted third‑party services (such as email delivery and analytics) that process data on our behalf. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or governmental authority, we may disclose information.
  • Protection of rights — if necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Gridline Home Ideas, our readers, or others.

5. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy:

  • Contact enquiries — retained for up to 12 months after resolution, unless a longer period is required for legal reasons.
  • Newsletter subscriptions — retained until you unsubscribe, after which your email is removed from our active list.
  • Analytics data — retained in aggregate form indefinitely, as it does not identify individuals.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal limitations.
  • Right to restrict processing — request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability — request a machine‑readable copy of data you provided to us.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at editors@gridlinehomeideas.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. These measures include encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and regular reviews of our practices.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International transfers

Gridline Home Ideas is based in Denmark. If you are accessing the Site from outside the EEA, your information may be transferred to and processed in Denmark or other countries where our service providers operate. We take steps to ensure that any international transfer of data complies with applicable data protection laws.

9. Children's privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

If we make significant changes, we will provide a more prominent notice on the Site.
